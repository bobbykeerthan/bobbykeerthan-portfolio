The Snake’s Promise

My MA final major project: a 72-second hand-drawn film that retells one Tamil couplet, around 2,000 years old, in an Indian folk-art style.

The Thirukkural has 1,330 couplets of just seven words each. I chose Kural 824, which warns you to fear people who smile sweetly while their heart is bitter. In my film, a man helps a snake that promises it won’t bite him. Every frame is hand-drawn in Clip Studio, and I voiced it myself. It was my first time doing both animation and voice.

“’Tis fitting you should dread dissemblers’ guile, Whose hearts are bitter while their faces smile.”
