Research & design

Temple murals, folk painting and a lot of drawing before the final look.

I started with storytelling: how games like Gris and A Plague Tale make you feel something without explaining it. Then I looked back home, at Sangam poetry, the murals at the Brihadeeswarar temple in Thanjavur, and Chola bronzes. I studied Madhubani, Gond, Warli and Kalamkari, and built my style from them: bold double outlines, flat colour, dots and no empty space. Madhubani gave me the eyes and patterns. My colour palettes come from temple stone, bronze, earth and textiles. My first character didn’t feel like folk art yet, so I kept drawing until he did. The story changed too: my first storyboard had a house, so I moved everything into the forest.
