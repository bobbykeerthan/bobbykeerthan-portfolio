Project 02 · Event, nightlife poster

After Dark

A local DJ club night needed a poster, quickly. Standing outside my accommodation one night, I took a photo of the street on my iPhone. I dropped the saturation and brilliance and pushed the contrast, and it suddenly looked like a poster. So I turned it into one for the event.
