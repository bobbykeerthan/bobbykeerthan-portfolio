Invisible Strings

“What controls you?” A black-and-white animated short.

A university project about the illusion of freedom. Everyone is controlled by something, not just me. Even I am controlled by rules and money. A puppet breaks free of its strings and wanders around using its own free will. Then a light flashes, it looks at the buildings and at its own hands, and sees that everything is still connected by strings. Something pulls it back up, and the film ends by asking you directly: what controls you? I used surrealism and Gestalt theory to show the feeling. Made in After Effects, and the string music was played and recorded by me.
