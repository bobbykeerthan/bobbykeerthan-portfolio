Sketch to film

A 77-second pencil animatic, trimmed to the 72-second film, then every shot redrawn in colour.

The animatic set the timing, and most of its shots made it into the film. My storyboard felt flat, so I took inspiration from anime. I started with seven camera angles and ended up with 18, cutting between the man’s worried face and the snake’s eye. The bite is an impact frame, which my professors singled out in my final presentation. I painted every asset in the same folk style: bushes, leaves, clouds, ground, stones and trees. Then I added sound, the kural read in Tamil and English subtitles. You feel the betrayal first, and the kural explains it at the end.

Next: one kural, one film. I want to make this a series.
