Project 01 · Portrait of a friend, slice-of-life poster

Single Player

I've been gaming since I was three. Here I found two friends who love games too, and every night after shifts or college we'd put a randomiser on and play one game till we finished: Expedition 33, A Plague Tale and loads more. They're single player games, but we play them together. The controller cable runs from my friend's hands and plugs straight into the title.

Tools

Autodesk SketchBook Pro (illustration) · Figma (poster design, night grade & type)

Role

Illustration, art direction, typography & poster design

Source

Developed from a photo I took of my friend mid-game.
